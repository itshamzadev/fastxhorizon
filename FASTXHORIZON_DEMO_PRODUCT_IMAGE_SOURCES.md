photographs. The source images are stored under `public/assets/demo-products/`
and copied into the existing 6Valley product storage paths by the demo catalog
setup.

## Specific product matches

Additional local assets are used when the product name is more specific than
its marketplace category:

- `fashion/shoes.webp` — sneakers and shoes
- `accessories/watch.webp` — watches
- `accessories/bag.webp` — bags, backpacks, and totes
- `beauty/perfume.webp` — perfumes and fragrances
- `home-living/kitchen.webp` — cookware and kitchen items

These are local optimized copies, not runtime hotlinks. The original source
platform is Unsplash; the images are used under the Unsplash license. They are
demo catalog imagery and can be replaced with merchant-owned product photos
later.
